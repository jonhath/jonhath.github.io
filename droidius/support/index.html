Droidius Support

Need help or found a bug? Email hathawaypixels@gmail.com. It's a small team, so give us a few days to reply. If you're reporting a bug, it helps to include your phone model, its OS version, and which level you were on.

FAQ

Is Droidius really free?
Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and nothing to unlock with money.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. Droidius plays entirely offline, including in airplane mode.
Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-in of any kind.
Will my progress carry over to a new phone, or between my phone and tablet?

Not through the game. Your unlocked levels, high scores, and settings are saved only on the device you're playing on. There's no cloud save. If you restore a new phone from a backup of your old one (Google backup on Android, iCloud or a computer backup on iOS), your phone may bring your game data along, but that depends on your phone's backup settings, not on Droidius. Progress doesn't move between Android and iOS.

Progress is also lost if you uninstall the game (unless your phone restores it from a backup).

I played the original 2011 Droidius. Where did my high scores go?
The remaster is a complete rebuild, and it can't read the old version's saved data, so scores and progress from the 2011 game don't carry over. Sorry! The upside is nine levels to beat instead of three.
How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings from the main menu and tap Clear Game Progress, then confirm. This resets your high scores, level unlocks, and Endless mode progress. It can't be undone. Your sound, vibration and other settings stay as they are.

To reset everything, including settings: on Android, go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Droidius → Storage → Clear storage. On iOS, delete the app and reinstall it.

Can I play a later level without beating the earlier ones?
Yes. Settings → Unlock All Levels opens every level, plus Endless mode.
My thumb covers the ship.
Make sure Finger Offset is turned on in Settings (it's on by default). It keeps the ship out from under your finger while you drag.
How do I turn off vibration, music or sound effects?
All three can be switched off separately in Settings.
What happens if I switch apps or take a call mid-level?
The game pauses automatically. When you come back, tap Resume on the pause menu.
Which devices are supported?
Android 7.1 or newer (64-bit devices), and iPhone or iPad running iOS 15 or newer.
Which languages are supported?
English.
