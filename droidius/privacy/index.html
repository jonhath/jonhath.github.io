Droidius Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026
Publisher: Jon Hathaway ("we", "us")

Droidius is a free game with no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and no online features. We wrote this policy to be short because there is very little to say.

What we collect

Nothing. Droidius does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting software, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your progress and settings, Droidius saves a small amount of game data only on your own device, using the standard app storage built into Android and iOS:

  • which levels you've unlocked, and whether Endless mode is unlocked
  • your high score for each level, your best Endless score, and how many Endless battles you've won
  • your settings: music and sound-effect volume and on/off, vibration, Finger Offset, power-up text, and level duration

This data never leaves your device through the game, and we can't see it. You can erase your progress at any time from Settings → Clear Game Progress in the game. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

If your phone's own backup feature is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud or computer backups on iOS), the operating system may include this game data in your backup, under your own account, the same as it does for other apps. We don't receive or have access to those backups.

App stores

You download Droidius through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Those stores handle downloads and may collect information under their own privacy policies. If you've chosen to share diagnostics with Google or Apple, they may give us aggregated, anonymized crash and performance statistics. That reporting is done by the store and your device's operating system, not by the game, and it doesn't identify you to us.

Children's privacy

Droidius is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we'll receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, we don't add you to any mailing list, and we don't share it with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future update adds a feature that needs data), we'll update this page and the effective date above before that update is released.
